Section 2

Key concepts, explained simply

These are the ideas that come up again and again. Open each one and make sure you could explain it to a friend.

Responsible AI

Microsoft's six principles are fairness, reliability and safety, privacy and security, inclusiveness, transparency, and accountability. Expect scenario questions that ask which principle applies.

Machine learning types

Regression predicts a number (such as a price). Classification predicts a category (such as spam or not spam). Clustering groups similar items without labels.

Computer vision

Image classification labels a whole image, object detection finds and boxes items in it, and OCR reads text from images.

Natural language processing

Common tasks are sentiment analysis, key phrase extraction, entity recognition, translation, and speech to text or text to speech.

Generative AI

Large language models generate text and code from prompts. Good results come from clear prompts and grounding the model in trusted data, with content filters for safety.

Section 3

Study plan

About an hour a day is enough. Adjust the pace to your experience.

  1. Week 1AI workloads and responsible AI principles.
  2. Week 2Machine learning basics, then computer vision and language workloads.
  3. Week 3Generative AI and hands-on practice in Microsoft Foundry.
  4. Week 4Review weak areas and take the free practice assessment.
Section 4

Exam tips

  • Read every question twice. Many answers are “which service fits this scenario”.
  • Use the free practice assessment on Microsoft Learn until you score 80% or more.
  • Hands-on practice beats memorising: build what you read about in a free account.
  • Skip hard questions and come back. Unanswered questions score zero, so answer everything.
  • Check the official study guide a week before your exam in case the skills changed.
